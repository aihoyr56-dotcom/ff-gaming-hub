🎯 Quick Gaming Tips

Simple habits that can help you play more consistently.

🎯

Practice your aim

Use training mode to practise short bursts and controlled movement before jumping into ranked matches.

🛡️

Use cover wisely

Move between safe positions, avoid standing still in open areas, and keep an escape route in mind.

🎧

Listen for clues

At a comfortable volume, pay attention to nearby movement and communicate useful information with teammates.

📚 Beginner Guides

Start with the basics and adjust your setup gradually.

📱

Find comfortable settings

Change one sensitivity or control setting at a time, test it in training, and keep what feels consistent on your device.

🤝

Play as a team

Share enemy locations, stay close enough to support teammates, and agree on a simple plan before a match.

🗺️

Learn the map

Remember useful cover, safe rotations, and places where you can find supplies without crossing open ground.

⚙️

Keep your phone ready

Close unused apps, keep enough storage available, and use a stable connection for smoother gameplay.
